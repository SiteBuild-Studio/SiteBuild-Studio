Our Story

Started as a stall, grew into a home.

Kettle & Vine began as a Saturday market coffee stall before finding a permanent spot on a quiet corner. The menu is still small on purpose — a short list, done properly, beats a long one done in a hurry.

Everything is made fresh each morning, and the kitchen closes when the ingredients for the day run out — not before, not after.
